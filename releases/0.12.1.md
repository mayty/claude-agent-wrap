## What's Changed

### Fixed: a live fleet no longer blocks `agent run` over an update you were never offered

The update check inside `agent run` and `agent rebuild` looked for running agent containers and sidecars before it asked whether to update. With anything running, the first invocation after an upstream release printed `update refused: N container(s) still running` and exited `1` — with no prompt, and for an update you had not asked for. The refusal now comes after the prompt: answering `n` (or Enter) launches or rebuilds exactly as it would with nothing to update, and only an update you accepted is refused while containers are live. `agent update` is unchanged, since asking for the update is the whole of that command.
