## What's Changed

### Fixed: `agent stats` shows Bedrock costs again

AWS changed the layout of its Bedrock pricing page. The wrapper then read no Claude prices from the page. `agent stats` used an old cached price table, or showed an unknown or `$0` cost for Bedrock usage. The wrapper now reads the new page layout, and `agent stats` prices each Bedrock model again.

**Full Changelog**: https://github.com/mayty/claude-agent-wrap/compare/0.12.1...0.12.2
